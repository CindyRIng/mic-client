Company Profile

Shenzhen Enrich Lighting Co., Ltd is a professional manufacturer specializing in high-quality LED lighting.

Established in 2008, we have over 18 years of industry experience and are headquartered in Shenzhen, with manufacturing facilities based in Foshan, Guangong.

To date, our products have been successfully exported to nearly 140 countries and regions, serving more than 10, 000 loyal customers worldwide.

Over the years, Enrich Lighting has earned recognition as a trusted brand known for quality, reliability, and innovation.

Since 2015, we have strategically expanded into modern commercial lighting, focus on LED Linear Lights. Our linear series delivers high performance, achieving up to 135-140 lm/W, with select models offering ultra-low glare (UGR < 19). These lights are available in rectangular, triangular, and square configurations, providing flexibility for diverse architectural needs.

In 2017, we introduced curved aluminum LED profiles, marking a significant advancement in design and aesthetics. Our curved LED lights come in circular, S-shaped, oval, hexagonal, and triangular forms-available in standard diameters from 0.45m to 7.0m, with full customization options for size and drawing. These products have become one of our core offerings, consistently re-ordered by international clients each year.

We offer multiple control options including On/Off, 0-10V, DALI, Triac, KNX, Casambi, and 2.4G dimming, along with RGB color-changing and tunable white (CCT) functions. Finishes are available in white, black, silver, gold, and custom RAL colors. Installation types include suspended, surface-mounted, and recessed solutions, ensuring adaptability across projects.

Responding to evolving market demands.

 since 2018 we have developed acoustic lighting solutions. By combining sound-absorbing materials with efficient illumination, our acoustic lights provide both functional and aesthetic value-receiving excellent feedback from global projects. Custom designs are also supported to meet specific project requirements.

Looking ahead to 2026, we are investing in R&D for sustainable lighting, including LED green plant lights and moss-integrated lighting systems, aiming to support eco-friendly interior environments and meet growing demand for biophilic design.

Our mission is simple: To deliver comprehensive, customer-driven lighting solutions that promote healthy spaces and create lasting value.

We would welcome the opportunity to explore potential collaboration with your esteemed company. Please feel free to contact us for further information, product catalogs, or technical support or sample requests. We look forward to building a long-term, mutually beneficial partnership in the global LED lighting market.
